Proven Tech Resume Writing Tips & Bullet Formulas

Transform vague task descriptions into compelling, quantifiable achievements that catch the eye of engineering hiring managers.

The Google "XYZ" Accomplishment Formula

Senior engineering recruiters look for impact, not just job descriptions. The standard formula popularized by former Google VP of People Operations Laszlo Bock is:

“Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]”

Eliminating Passive Weak Phrases

Recruiters scan each bullet in 3 to 6 seconds. Passive opening words immediately dilute the perceived impact of your contribution:

Weak Passive Phrase High-Impact Action Verb Why It Matters
"Worked on backend API" Architected & Deployed RESTful API Demonstrates ownership and end-to-end delivery
"Responsible for database bugs" Optimized SQL Indexing & Reduced Latency Proves measurable technical competence
"Helped team with Docker" Containerized Microservices Pipeline Highlights collaboration through tangible tooling

How to Quantify Engineering Bullets Without Confidential Numbers

Students often feel they don't have corporate financial metrics to share. You can still quantify technical outcomes:

  • Performance Metrics: "Decreased query response time from 450ms to 110ms."
  • Scale / Volume: "Processed over 25,000 JSON events per hour during load testing."
  • Efficiency: "Automated manual test suites, cutting regression execution from 2 hours to 8 minutes."
  • Test Coverage: "Increased backend unit test coverage from 42% to 88% using JUnit and Mockito."
